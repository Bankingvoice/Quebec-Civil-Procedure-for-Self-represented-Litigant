# Architecture of Quebec's Code of Civil Procedure (CQLR, c. C-25.01)

## Overview of the 8 Books

| Book | Title in C.C.P. | Plain-Language Purpose for a Litigant | Key Articles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Book I** | General Framework of Civil Procedure | Guiding principles (mediation first, proportionality, cooperation), which court has jurisdiction, how to write and serve pleadings, and how to count deadlines. | Arts. 1–140 |
| **Book II** | Contentious Proceedings | Step-by-step lifecycle of a contested lawsuit: Summons, 15-day Answer, 45-day Case Protocol, preliminary exceptions, pre-trial discovery, evidence, and trial. | Arts. 141–301 |
| **Book III** | Procedure in Non-Contentious Proceedings | Uncontested applications where there is no opponent (e.g., homologating a protection mandate, probating a will, tutorship). | Arts. 302–320 |
| **Book IV** | Judgment, Application for Revocation and Appeal | How judgments are issued, legal costs, how to ask a court to cancel a default judgment (revocation), and how to appeal to the Court of Appeal. | Arts. 321–390 |
| **Book V** | Rules Applicable to Certain Civil Matters | Special procedural rules for family law (divorce, custody, child support), personal status/integrity, estates, property, and international disputes. | Arts. 391–508 |
| **Book VI** | Special Procedures | Emergency orders (injunctions, safeguard orders, seizures before judgment), **Simplified Procedure** (< $100k in Court of Québec), **Small Claims** (≤ $15k), and class actions. | Arts. 509–604 |
| **Book VII** | Private Dispute Prevention and Resolution | Rules governing mediation and private arbitration outside court. | Arts. 605–655 |
| **Book VIII** | Execution of Judgments | How to collect money or enforce an order after winning: bailiff instructions, garnishing wages/bank accounts, and seizing/selling property. | Arts. 656–777 |

## Choosing the Right Quebec Forum

| Forum | Monetary / Subject Scope | Lawyer Rule | Key Procedural Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small Claims Division** (Court of Québec) | Claims up to **$15,000** principal (before interest). Excludes family law, residential leases, and defamation. | **No lawyers** represent parties at the hearing (art. 542 C.C.P.). | Form SJ-870E; clerk serves defendant; mandatory mediation if ≤ $5,000; informal hearing; **no appeal** (art. 564). |
| **Court of Québec (Civil Division)** | Exclusive: **$15,000.01 to < $75,000**.<br>Concurrent with Superior Court: **$75,000 to < $100,000** (art. 35 C.C.P.). | Individuals may self-represent; corporations must use a lawyer. | Claims < $100,000 use **Simplified Rules** (arts. 535.1–535.15): 5-page limit on originating application, no case protocol, no discovery if < $50k. |
| **Superior Court of Québec** | **$100,000 or more**, plus exclusive jurisdiction over family law, injunctions, judicial review, and class actions (art. 33 C.C.P.). | Individuals may self-represent; corporations must use a lawyer. | Full formal procedure: Summons, 45-day Case Protocol, written/oral discovery, settlement conference, formal trial. |
| **Court of Appeal of Québec** | Appeals from final judgments of Superior Court and Court of Québec (excluding Small Claims). | Individuals may self-represent; corporations must use a lawyer. | Strict **30-day deadline** to file Notice of Appeal or Application for Leave to Appeal (art. 360 C.C.P.). |
| **Tribunal administratif du logement (TAL)** | Residential landlord-tenant disputes (unpaid rent, eviction, repairs, lease termination). | Self-representation common; lawyers allowed. | Separate administrative tribunal; do not file residential lease claims in Small Claims. |

*Note: Monetary thresholds in art. 35 C.C.P. are subject to statutory indexation. Always confirm current indexed ceilings on LégisQuébec (c. C-25.01) before filing.*
